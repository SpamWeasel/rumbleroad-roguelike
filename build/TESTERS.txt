RUMBLE ROW 0.9 - EARLY TEST BUILD (please read me)
==================================================

Thanks for testing Rumble Row! It's an ASCII street-brawling roguelike. The gangs have taken
over the city: eat well, train hard, and fight your way east to Duke Varga in Crown Tower.


NEW IN 0.9 - "RAIN OR SHINE"
----------------------------
- Weather: every day is clear, rainy, foggy or a heatwave (the icon by the clock shows which).
    Rain: slick streets send everyone flying a square further, you see less, restaurant
          meals heal 50% more, and the ice cream cart stays home.
    Fog: you can't see far, but gangs don't spot you until you're close.
    Heatwave: fewer gang crews out, and cold drinks restore twice the guts.
  Indoors (the subway, the Kennel, Crown Tower, fight clubs) it's always dry.
- Street Guide (press F): a menu card of every dish in the city, ticked off as you try them,
  and a guide to every kind of gang member and boss you've beaten. Two new achievements:
  Food Critic and Field Guide.
- Look mode (press V): move a cursor around the map to see what anything is, without a mouse.
  On a controller: View + Y. (View + X opens the Street Guide.)
- Tips for new players: a short tip the first time something comes up (your first gang member,
  bleeding, night, rain...). Each shows once; switch them off in Settings.
- Balance checked on 12 games per difficulty: Easy, Normal and Hard now line up as they should.
- Your 0.8 save carries over.


NEW IN 0.8 - "WORD ON THE STREET"
----------------------------------
- Side quests: eight people around the city need a hand (Rosa, Coach Brannigan, Salty Pete,
  Old Mae, Big Mike, Jinx at the arcade, Mrs. Hartwell and Margot). They stand outside their
  shops, highlighted in yellow - walk into them to talk. Quests send you after named fighters,
  gang clean-ups, fight-club bouts, deliveries and errands. Most rewards add a secret dish to
  that shop's menu for good. Press U for your quest log.
- New kinds of gang members, more of them deeper into the city:
    k knife fighters - their cuts keep bleeding until you eat or use a heal
    m medics - they patch up hurt friends and stay out of reach: take them out first
    r riders - they charge in from a distance in a straight line
    g guards - they block punches from the front: kick them, or get around the side
- Some gang members have a trait: Tough, Quick, Loaded (lots of cash) or Loud (wakes the block).
  Point the mouse at anyone to see what they do.
- Each boss has a short scene the first time they spot you.
- Beating Duke Varga now ends with an epilogue that reflects your run: the turf you held, the
  people you helped, your partner and your rival.
- Two new achievements for side quests.
- Your 0.7 save carries over.


NEW IN 0.7 - "TURF WAR"
------------------------
- Sunset Pier (head down the boardwalk from Harbor Market): a carnival with a pier grill,
  a sweets stand, and two games you play with a timing meter. Ring the bell on the High
  Striker or shoot ducks in the Shooting Gallery to win tickets, then trade them at the Prize
  Booth for prizes like the Giant Stuffed Gorilla and the Ringmaster's Jacket.
- Lantern Row (up the alley off the Neon Strip): a night market. Most stalls only open after
  dark: lamb skewers, hot pot, jade charms, and Madame Velvet's fortunes (a fortune boosts a
  random stat for a while, and now and then for good). Boba Bros and the herbalist open by day.
- Turf: knock out every gang member in a street district on 3 different visits and it
  becomes your turf. Your blocks have fewer gang crews and 20% cheaper street carts, and
  every dawn (5:00) the shopkeepers there chip in some cash.
- Raids: some mornings a gang moves back into one of your districts. Beat the raiders before
  the next dawn, or you lose the block (and have to clear it out again).
- City map (press N, or from the Esc menu): every area you've found, who runs it, the bosses
  you've beaten, and your turf.
- Daily runs: pick "Daily run" when you start a new game and you get the same city as
  everyone else playing that day. Compare runs with friends! Records mark daily runs.
- Six new achievements: turf, raids, daily runs, ringing the bell, and the grand prize.
- Your 0.6 save carries over.


NEW IN 0.6 - "UPTOWN"
----------------------
- The Kennel: the Chrome Dogs finally have a hideout, in an old subway depot past the
  Underline, and a boss: Kilo Marsh, who pounces, mauls and howls for the pack.
- Ashgate Heights, a fourth town between the Kennel and Crown Tower:
    Chez Margot and Saffron House (the best food in the city), Fenwick's Fine Foods,
    the Ashgate Grand hotel, a job board, a bus stop, and a hidden fight club (The Vault).
- The Summit Athletic Club teaches two new moves: Rising Knee (crushes foes who are down or
  dazed) and Counter Stance (slip punches and hit back for a few turns). Felix Duarte, a
  gentleman boxer, will team up with you there.
- Hartwell & Daughters, Tailors: have the gear you're wearing improved, up to three times.
- Second Chance Pawn: second-hand gear at a discount plus one rare find (the cases change
  every visit), and it buys things from your pockets.
- Duke Varga and his Crown Tower guards are tougher, to match.
- Controller: hold View and press RB / LB to walk to the next / previous area.
- Three new achievements, including holding all four fight club belts.
- Fixed a rare "could not save" error when two saves happened at the same moment.
- Your 0.5 save carries over. If you're already past the subway, Kilo won't block the way.


NEW IN 0.5 - "CITY NIGHTS"
--------------------------
- Day and night: the clock (top right) runs a minute per move. From 21:00 to 5:00 the streets
  are darker, gang crews are bigger and carry more cash, and some shops close (the bakery,
  bookstore, juice bar, tea stand and some carts). A shady dealer only comes out at night.
  Napping at home after dark sleeps until morning.
- Bus stops (a blue B in each town) take you to any town you've already reached, for a few
  dollars. No more walking back through cleared streets.
- Quick travel: ] walks to the next area's exit and [ walks back toward town. It stops by itself
  when someone shows up.
- Achievements (on the title screen), kept across all your games. Two of them unlock new
  fighting styles: the Wrestler and the Night Runner.
- Your 0.4 save carries over.


NEW IN 0.4 - "CREW"
-------------------
- Partners: every gym has a fighter who'll team up with you (gym > Partner tab). They follow
  you, fight beside you and grow as you do. They keep 10% of what the gangs drop, and if
  they're knocked out they meet you in the next town. One partner at a time.
- Combos: three punches or kicks in a row on the same foe end in a COMBO finisher that knocks
  them flying and down.
- New Game+: beat Duke Varga and start a new city with everything you've earned. The gangs come
  back much tougher each round.
- Character sheet (press P): your stats and where they come from, moves, partner and records.
- Your 0.3 save carries over.


NEW IN 0.3 - "STREET LIFE"
--------------------------
- Fighting styles: start as an All-Rounder, Boxer, Street Kicker, Brawler or Hustler.
  Each has its own stats and a perk.
- Job boards in every town (a yellow ≡ door): bounties on wanted fighters, gang clean-ups and
  parcel deliveries. Hold up to 3 at once; press O to see yours. You're paid on the spot.
- Street events: rescue people being shaken down by gangs, survive ambushes, find lost cash.
- Your rival, Dex Marlowe, turns up on the streets now and then, a little stronger each time.
  Beat him five times for a prize.
- Chiptune music for the title, towns, streets, fight clubs and boss fights, with its own
  volume in Settings.
- Your 0.2 save carries over (as an All-Rounder).


NEW IN 0.2
----------
- The game saves as you play and when you close the window - no more lost progress.
- 3 save slots, with automatic backups. A damaged save loads its newest backup instead.
- Settings: sound volume, text size, screen shake, fullscreen, and change any key.
- Controller support (Xbox-style pads): plug one in and play.
- Easy / Normal / Hard difficulty.
- Hidden fight clubs in every town (look for a red ! door). Bet on yourself, win purses,

- Gang members now pick up pipes, bats and trash cans and use them on you.
- Daily specials: every restaurant has one dish at 40% off, changing each visit.
- Point the mouse at anything to see what it is (enemy health, today's special...).
- Records screen with your best runs, and "Copy a bug report" in the Esc menu.
- Bigger hits shake the screen; whoever hits you flashes orange.
- Your 0.1 save carries over into slot 1.


STARTING THE GAME
-----------------
- Zip download: unzip it anywhere (right-click > Extract All), open the folder, and
  double-click RumbleRow.exe. Nothing is installed.
- Portable .exe: double-click it. It takes a few seconds to start each time.
- Installer: run it; it adds Rumble Row to the Start menu and the desktop.

Pick the file for your PC: "x64" for most Windows PCs (Intel or AMD), "arm64" for Windows on
ARM (Snapdragon / Surface Pro X style laptops). If unsure, use x64 - it runs everywhere.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC", because the game isn't signed yet. Click
"More info", then "Run anyway".


HOW TO PLAY
-----------
Press ? in the game for every control (keys can be changed in Settings). The basics:

  Q W E / A D / Z X C ... move (diagonals too), or arrow keys / numpad
  S ..................... wait a turn
  J / K ................. punch / kick (or just walk into a gang member)
  1 - 9 ................. special moves you've learned at a gym
  Space ................. guard
  G / T ................. pick up / throw crates, trash cans, pipes...
  I ..................... pockets (snacks, bandages, firecrackers)
  R ..................... rest      O ..... your jobs      P ... character sheet
  N ..................... city map      U ..... side quests   F ... Street Guide
  V ..................... look around
  ] / [ ................. walk to the next exit / back toward town
  Esc ................... menu      F11 ... fullscreen

Controller: stick or d-pad moves, A punch, B kick, X special, Y guard, LB wait,
RB next special, LT grab, RT throw, View pockets, Menu button for the menu,
View + RB / LB walk to the next / previous area.

Walk into a shop door to go in. Food heals you AND permanently raises your stats, but each
dish only helps up to a point - so try the menus in every new town. Gyms teach special moves.

Knock gang members into walls, into each other, or into the water. Big guys wind up huge
swings first - the square they're aiming at flashes red. Step away or guard!


YOUR SAVES
----------
Saves live in %APPDATA%\RumbleRow\Saves (one file per slot, plus a Backups folder).
Deleting or moving the game never touches them.


REPORTING A PROBLEM
-------------------
1. Press Esc in the game and choose "Copy a bug report", then paste it into your message.
2. Say what you were doing and what went wrong.

Also handy: how far you got, how long it took, which difficulty, and where it felt unfair.
