RUMBLE ROW 0.4 - EARLY TEST BUILD (please read me)
==================================================

Thanks for testing Rumble Row! It's an ASCII street-brawling roguelike. The gangs have taken
over the city: eat well, train hard, and fight your way east to Duke Varga in Crown Tower.


NEW IN 0.4 - "CREW"
-------------------
- Partners: every gym has a fighter who'll team up with you (gym > Partner tab). They follow
  you, fight beside you and grow as you do. They keep 10% of what the gangs drop, and if
  they're knocked out they meet you in the next town. One partner at a time.
- Combos: three punches or kicks in a row on the same foe end in a COMBO finisher that knocks
  them flying and down.
- New Game+: beat Duke Varga and start a new city with everything you've earned. The gangs come
  back much tougher each round.
- Character sheet (press P): your stats and where they come from, moves, partner and records.
- Your 0.3 save carries over.


NEW IN 0.3 - "STREET LIFE"
--------------------------
- Fighting styles: start as an All-Rounder, Boxer, Street Kicker, Brawler or Hustler.
  Each has its own stats and a perk.
- Job boards in every town (a yellow ≡ door): bounties on wanted fighters, gang clean-ups and
  parcel deliveries. Hold up to 3 at once; press O to see yours. You're paid on the spot.
- Street events: rescue people being shaken down by gangs, survive ambushes, find lost cash.
- Your rival, Dex Marlowe, turns up on the streets now and then, a little stronger each time.
  Beat him five times for a prize.
- Chiptune music for the title, towns, streets, fight clubs and boss fights, with its own
  volume in Settings.
- Your 0.2 save carries over (as an All-Rounder).


NEW IN 0.2
----------
- The game saves as you play and when you close the window - no more lost progress.
- 3 save slots, with automatic backups. A damaged save loads its newest backup instead.
- Settings: sound volume, text size, screen shake, fullscreen, and change any key.
- Controller support (Xbox-style pads): plug one in and play.
- Easy / Normal / Hard difficulty.
- Hidden fight clubs in every town (look for a red ! door). Bet on yourself, win purses,
  and take the title belts from three champions.
- Gang members now pick up pipes, bats and trash cans and use them on you.
- Daily specials: every restaurant has one dish at 40% off, changing each visit.
- Point the mouse at anything to see what it is (enemy health, today's special...).
- Records screen with your best runs, and "Copy a bug report" in the Esc menu.
- Bigger hits shake the screen; whoever hits you flashes orange.
- Your 0.1 save carries over into slot 1.


STARTING THE GAME
-----------------
- Zip download: unzip it anywhere (right-click > Extract All), open the folder, and
  double-click RumbleRow.exe. Nothing is installed.
- Portable .exe: double-click it. It takes a few seconds to start each time.
- Installer: run it; it adds Rumble Row to the Start menu and the desktop.

Pick the file for your PC: "x64" for most Windows PCs (Intel or AMD), "arm64" for Windows on
ARM (Snapdragon / Surface Pro X style laptops). If unsure, use x64 - it runs everywhere.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC", because the game isn't signed yet. Click
"More info", then "Run anyway".


HOW TO PLAY
-----------
Press ? in the game for every control (keys can be changed in Settings). The basics:

  Q W E / A D / Z X C ... move (diagonals too), or arrow keys / numpad
  S ..................... wait a turn
  J / K ................. punch / kick (or just walk into a gang member)
  1 - 9 ................. special moves you've learned at a gym
  Space ................. guard
  G / T ................. pick up / throw crates, trash cans, pipes...
  I ..................... pockets (snacks, bandages, firecrackers)
  R ..................... rest      O ..... your jobs      P ... character sheet
  Esc ................... menu      F11 ... fullscreen

Controller: stick or d-pad moves, A punch, B kick, X special, Y guard, LB wait,
RB next special, LT grab, RT throw, View pockets, Menu button for the menu.

Walk into a shop door to go in. Food heals you AND permanently raises your stats, but each
dish only helps up to a point - so try the menus in every new town. Gyms teach special moves.

Knock gang members into walls, into each other, or into the water. Big guys wind up huge
swings first - the square they're aiming at flashes red. Step away or guard!


YOUR SAVES
----------
Saves live in %APPDATA%\RumbleRow\Saves (one file per slot, plus a Backups folder).
Deleting or moving the game never touches them.


REPORTING A PROBLEM
-------------------
1. Press Esc in the game and choose "Copy a bug report", then paste it into your message.
2. Say what you were doing and what went wrong.

Also handy: how far you got, how long it took, which difficulty, and where it felt unfair.
